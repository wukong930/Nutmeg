## Recommendation

MCMC posterior mean predictions outperformed DC MLE by ≥ 5 milli-log-loss points on this single season (R-hat in [1.05, 1.10] — borderline, confirm with NUTS before any production decision). This is a candidate for production migration, but the delta is small enough to be noise on one season. **Recommendation: confirm on 2-3 additional held-out seasons (and switch from hand-rolled MH → NumPyro NUTS for cleaner posterior coverage) before investing in the PyMC/NumPyro dependency footprint.** If the gap holds, NumPyro (JAX-accelerated NUTS) would be ~10× faster than the MH used here and would push R-hat well below 1.05 in the same wall-clock budget.
